# Natural material studies: tracks 13–16

These Realism studies use generated photographic-style materials with local shader motion. They are not recorded footage or generated video.

- Prelude BWV 846: dew on a fragment of spider silk; supported-edge tension and locally lagging motion echo the repeated arpeggio.
- Pavane: a small shell remains fixed in wet sand while a shallow film of water and broken foam edge advances and retreats.
- Nocturne No. 20: a single close ice crystal drifts and turns within a dark field with small, defocused distant snow. Its appearance is an artistic interpretation, not a claim of scientific crystal accuracy.
- Pathétique, second movement: a young leaf bends gently from its fixed petiole, with local vein-related motion and transmitted light.

Each scene is distinct in material, composition and motion. The existing Original art and all other Realism scenes are unchanged. Materials load only when their scene is selected. The shared context and existing playback clock control rendering, including stop and reduced-motion behavior. Unsupported rendering uses the existing fallback.

Validation separates exact native shader rendering, API/unit tests, remote file verification and browser/device checks. Native rendering is not evidence of measured mobile GPU performance or actual audio playback.
