# Hidden threads in wet wood — editorial collaboration

Scene: `tchaikovsky-october` (26). Source: https://djnaoyaman.github.io/earthnumber/#fungus

The exhibition invites attention to fungal structures extending beyond their visible above-ground forms. This newly generated photographic-style forest-floor composition looks into naturally peeled wet bark and irregular pale fungal-like fibers. It is an editorial association, not the composer's intention, a photograph of an actual site, a species identification, or a reconstruction of the large individual described in the exhibition.

The wood and fibers remain fixed. Local rainwater gathers at a bark edge and detaches as a small drop. This is generated material with shader animation, not recorded footage, a generated video, fungal growth, or a calibrated hydrological model. The original mushroom/spore scene is replaced for this work alone; other scenes, Original, music information, controls, bounded material lifecycle and frame isolation are preserved.
