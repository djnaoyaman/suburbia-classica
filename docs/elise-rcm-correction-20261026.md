# Narrow factual correction: Für Elise difficulty source

The previous difficulty sentence incorrectly identified the grading institution as British and described its structure as ten stages. The Royal Conservatory of Music is based in Toronto, Canada. Its official Piano Syllabus 2022 lists Für Elise, WoO 59, in Level 7 (printed pages 60 and64; PDF indices59 and63). The syllabus also includes preparatory levels and diploma levels, so the simplified ten-stage claim is removed.

The correction retains the Level7 finding and the separate Henle reseller-based intermediate classification and its unconfirmed numeric rating. No other historical, performance, recording or artistic claims are changed. Two primary sources are added to the existing source lists.
