# Natural material studies: tracks 25–28

Generated photographic-style materials and independently evolving natural motion form these editorial interpretations. They are not recorded footage, generated videos or historical depictions. No species-level or scientific simulation accuracy is claimed.

- Farewell Waltz: small loose frost grains leave an anchored, frost-covered bark surface, interpreting the quiet separation suggested by the piece's familiar title.
- October, Autumn Song: fine motes drift from beneath a woodland mushroom, with its gills and surrounding log held still.
- Mozart K.310, second movement: slowly folding viscous material moves between dark volcanic rock, an interpretation of contrasting warmth and shadow rather than a setting associated with the composer.
- Schubert Impromptu D.899 No.3: internal resin detail travels slowly along a fixed natural channel, accompanying the continuous flow of the broken-chord texture.

Only these four Realism entries are added. Original artwork, earlier scenes, song data, controls and always-visible metadata are retained. Materials are selected lazily, using the shared context, logical animation clock, stop/reduced-motion behavior and existing fallback.

Native rendering checks are distinct from actual browser/device performance and audio-playback checks.
