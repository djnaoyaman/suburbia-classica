## Hanging-shape correction

The previous cutout had a lifted diagonal hem and unsupported sail-like bulge. The replacement alpha material hangs in substantial vertical pleats, with a nearly level hem close to the stage floor. The upper support and left hanging edge are pinned. Minute local fold drift replaces the former large wind displacement; vertical movement is especially restrained. A small fabric-only light variation follows the folds. The complete slow pattern repeats after60seconds, using caller time only.

The original stone arch, backstage rigging and floor backdrop remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Background coordinates are never deformed, including gaps exposed by the curtain's small edge movement. Static responsive framing retains the hem and stage floor in wide views and the cloth edge plus arch on phones. No animated pan, scene-wide warp or full-screen illumination pulse is used.

The new curtain has a new filename to avoid stale material caches; the older asset remains unused. Two-texture readiness/cache/release, context restoration, failed-upload fallback and non-Carmen delegation retain the existing shared lifecycle. Pause, seek and reduced-motion behavior continue to be controlled by the existing host clock.
