# Natural material studies: tracks 17–20

Generated photographic-style materials are combined with locally evolving shader motion. These are artistic interpretations of the music, not recorded footage, generated videos or depictions of historical events.

- Mozart K.331, first movement: fine water strands pass down a detailed mineral face. The recurring streams and changes in their optical detail accompany the theme-and-variations idea.
- Chopin Prelude No.7: delicate vapor rises from a small warm pool. Water and vapor move locally while the natural setting stays anchored.
- Brahms Waltz No.15: an autumn leaf drifts in a slow rotational current, with water reflections and contact detail.
- Mendelssohn Spring Song: small flowers and buds move in slightly different phases along a branch, responding to the light, decorative musical character.

Only these four Realism scenes are added. Original artwork, all previous scenes, song data and controls are retained. Materials load on selection; rendering uses the existing shared context, logical clock, stop and reduced-motion behavior, with fallback on unsupported or failed rendering.

Native shader and API tests are distinct from actual browser/device performance and audio-playback checks.
