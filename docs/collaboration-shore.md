# Scene 24 — the passage beside the sea

Chopin, Waltz Op. 64 No. 2.

The new generated photographic-style coastal scene draws on [鎌・くらんぽ: 新田義貞、稲村ヶ崎伝説](https://djnaoyaman.github.io/kuranpo/knowledge/g13.html), which reads a legend through the landscape while explicitly distinguishing that legend from verified history. A narrow wet sandy strip follows a low rock shelf; ordinary gentle waves cover and reveal the margin between land and water.

This is an editorial creation, not a photograph or accurate reconstruction of Inamuragasaki, and not a depiction of a historical miracle. No sword, dragon, warrior or miraculous divided sea appears. The short animation concerns wave runup and retreat, not a 48-second tidal cycle. The source material is newly generated and locally animated by a shader, not recorded or generated video.
