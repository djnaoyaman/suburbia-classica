# Verdi — Dies irae: continuous volcanic ash

The existing uninhabited volcano photograph and composition remain unchanged. The volcano is an editorial metaphor for the music, not a documented place associated with Verdi or footage of an eruption.

The previous small interior motion is replaced by a plume-shaped local flow: ash detail rises, drifts slightly and folds into the next billow. Two offset 14-second advection phases blend through their resets; slower wisps soften the upper ash toward nearby sky. The complete pattern repeats smoothly after 168 seconds. Terrain, crater and everything outside the feathered plume envelope remain fixed. There is no camera pan, whole-image warp or global illumination pulse.

The existing shared material lifecycle, image asset, focal crop, caller clock and fallback remain intact. Pause, seek and reduced motion continue to be controlled by the existing scene clock; this module adds no animation timer. Other Realism scenes, Visual, Impressive and audio are untouched.

This is an animated photographic material, not a volumetric or physical fluid simulation. Focused checks cover delegation, fixed material, timing input, load failures, context restoration and texture eviction. Native software-rendered shader frames are checked at wide, phone, ultrawide and square ratios for mask confinement, repeatability and phase continuity; these are not physical-device performance measurements.
