# Scene 29 — a path across the boundary

Brahms, Intermezzo Op. 118 No. 2.

This new generated photographic-style woodland interprets the editor’s view of mountains as a boundary beyond familiar village life in [山と妖怪](https://djnaoyaman.github.io/youkai/yama-yokai.html). A sunlit, walked path enters moss, exposed roots and deeper forest. Low mist moves locally across the continuing path, partly concealing and revealing it. Trees, roots and the path remain geometrically fixed.

This is an editorial creation, not a photograph of an actual place, a reconstruction of a legend, a documented supernatural event, or a claim about the composer’s intention. The material is newly generated, not reused from the former meadow scene. Motion is shader-based; it is neither recorded footage nor generated video.

Only this Realism artwork and its directly related connection copy change. Other 73 artworks, Original, music information, controls, bounded material loading and frame isolation remain unchanged. The former image is retained in repository history and its material path is not overwritten.
