# Visual batch 03 — four additional works

This increment brings Visual to 12 of the existing 74 tracks. It preserves the current Impressive and Realism modes, all recordings and metadata, the eight previously released Visual sources, and the shared lifecycle wrapper. Only the Visual catalog and its cache URL change at runtime.

## Editorial compositions

- **Raindrop:** three fixed response origins reshape a continuous field; broad pulse packets change their context. This is an interpretation of the persistent repeated pitch, not a claim to reconstruct rain or track individual notes.
- **Träumerei:** open curved spans overlap, with finer internal contours giving depth to returning gestures. No photographic cloth or static pastel silhouette is used.
- **Liebestraum No. 3:** a sustained branching luminous region contains finer articulated lines; the relative spacing and light change continuously. An opaque initial repaint prevents fractional scanline residue.
- **The Girl with the Flaxen Hair:** five asymmetric light regions contain differently directed fine laminations. Two quiet negative-space tiles are intentional; activity across the entire image at every instant is not claimed.

These are independent procedural compositions informed by the existing music notes. Colors and geometry are editorial choices, not scientific pitch-to-color mappings or statements of the composers' intentions.

## Verification and limits

The reviewed source text is embedded unchanged. Native Canvas tests cover five aspect ratios, deterministic seeks, continuous 24-second sequences and loop boundaries. The integrated wrapper is compared pixel-for-pixel against each original module, including prior batches. Existing pause, seek, reduced-motion, unsupported-track fallback and late-image guards are retained.

Visual follows the site's available playback-position clock; it does not obtain raw YouTube audio, FFT, MIDI or note events. Dense moving contours can shimmer. Native and desktop checks do not certify physical-device performance or medical safety. Impressive paintings, Realism materials, music data, collaboration credits and OGP assets are not modified.
