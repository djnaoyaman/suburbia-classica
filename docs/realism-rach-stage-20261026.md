# Rachmaninoff Piano Concerto No. 2, II — dawn lake

The Realism scene for `rach-pc2-2` is now an expansive dawn lake with receding low wooded islands, replacing the literal orchestral stage. Silver and restrained rose water, open depth and low mist translate the movement’s sustained lyrical breadth into a coherent landscape, not an isolated instrument.

## Musical grounding

The [Indianapolis Symphony Orchestra’s program note](https://www.indianapolissymphony.org/backstage/program-notes/rachmaninoff-concerto-no-2-in-c-minor/) identifies the second movement as *Adagio sostenuto*: the gentle theme passes from flute to clarinet and piano, and tranquil material returns at the close. [LA Phil’s note](https://www.laphil.com/works/piano-concerto-no-2-in-c-minor-op-18) describes its slow pastoral character. Dawn and the imagined lake are editorial metaphors for that lyricism, not a claim about Rachmaninoff’s travels, inspiration or a particular location.
