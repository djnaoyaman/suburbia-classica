# Natural material studies: tracks 21–24

These scenes combine generated photographic-style materials with locally evolving natural motion. They are editorial interpretations of the music, not recorded footage, generated videos or historical reconstructions.

- Goldberg Variations, Aria: quiet distant hills stay anchored beneath slowly changing thin night clouds, accompanying the calm sarabande character.
- Rêverie: translucent aquatic leaves bend from fixed roots while refracted light passes over the riverbed, an interpretation of the gentle melody.
- Gnossienne No.1: a fine layer of sand travels along a dune crest with uneven local movement, responding to the piece's freely unfolding time.
- Waltz Op.64 No.2: sea foam curls between dark stones and recedes in intertwined rhythms, accompanying the returning contrasting themes.

Only these four Realism entries are added. Original artwork, existing scenes, song data, controls and information-display behavior are retained. Materials load on selection and use the shared rendering context, stopped logical clock and reduced-motion behavior. Unsupported or failed rendering falls back to existing artwork.

Native shader and API validation are not claims of actual-browser performance, physical-device testing or audio playback.
