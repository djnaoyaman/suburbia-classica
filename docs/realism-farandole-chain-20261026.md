# Farandole — Sagrada Família interior

This owner-selected Realism setting is a generated photographic-style interpretation of the Basilica of the Sagrada Família in Barcelona. The branching column rhythms, successive spaces and overlapping stained-glass colors supply an editorial association with the music's interwoven energy. It is not a claim that Bizet or Guiraud visited, performed at, or drew inspiration from the Basilica. It is not a historical production scene, measured reconstruction or live photograph.

## Architectural grounding

The Basilica's own [Interior information booklet, pages4–8](https://sagradafamilia.org/documents/20142/1693659/SF_Booklet_09_20260227_digital_AF.pdf/96d648e4-1172-58bd-fa7b-886ebb516b36) describes inclined branching supports, a forest-like interior, hyperboloid light openings and the stained-glass color scheme. The [official photo gallery](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/web/guest/photo-gallery), including “Inside the Temple”, “Stained-glass windows and vaults” and “The forest of inclined trunks and branches”, supplies the visual architectural reference. The new asset is independently generated, not a republished gallery photograph; its perspective and lighting are interpretive.

## Framing and restrained motion

The image presents a deep grand interior, not isolated objects or dancers. Wide views include colored windows and floor light; phone framing follows the receding column rows and keeps the nave legible. Camera, column geometry, glass and floor sample fixed photograph coordinates. There is no pan, zoom or surface deformation.

A slow, low-amplitude attenuation affects only an existing colored floor-light pool. A few tiny soft motes drift in one narrow illuminated gap between pillars, fading fully before recycling. No scene-wide brightening, hue wave, fast sparkle or moving architecture is introduced.
